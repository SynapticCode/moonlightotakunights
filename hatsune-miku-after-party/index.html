よくある質問

FAQ

When and where is this?

Thursday, May 7, 2026. 10 PM to 3 AM. QXT's Nightclub, 248 Mulberry St, Newark NJ. Two blocks south of Prudential Center.

How do I get the $10 entry?

Show one of three things at the door — a Hatsune Miku EXPO 2026 ticket stub, full cosplay, or a college ID. Any one of the three gets you in for $10 all night. Otherwise it's $15 before 11:30 PM and $20 after.

Is this an official Hatsune Miku event?

No. This is an unofficial fan after-party hosted independently by Moonlight Otaku Nights and Miku Only NYC. We are not affiliated with Crypton Future Media, Yamaha Corporation, or any official Hatsune Miku entity.

What's the dress code?

There isn't one. Cosplay gets you the $10 door rate — Vocaloid, anime, original characters, whatever you've built. Not in cosplay? Show up in whatever feels like you. Goth, cyber, emo, punk, streetwear, jeans and a t-shirt. QXT's is an alternative club.

Can I leave and come back?

Yes. Re-entry is allowed all night. Tell security on your way out so they know to let you back in.

When do tickets go on sale?

Soon. Join the waitlist above and you'll get the link before they go public.
