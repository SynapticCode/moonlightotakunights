Looking for idol routines, dance covers, vocaloid performers, and stage acts that hit the anime-nightlife crowd. Solo or group, both work.

If you've ever performed at a con and thought "this crowd gets it, but I wish the production was actually good" — keep reading.

Moonlight is the first venue in Newark that's been built specifically for anime-adjacent live performance. Real stage. Real lighting rig. Real sound system tuned for vocals, not just bass.

What we book:

  • Dance covers — K-pop, J-pop, vocaloid, anime OPs. Solo or group up to 12.
  • Idol routines — original or cover, in-character or stage-name, with or without backing vocals.
  • Vocal performance — vocaloid covers, anime OST covers, original anime-influenced material.
  • Theatrical / stage acts — short narrative routines, lightsaber dueling, prop-based performance, host acts.
Be honest about your level. We book first-time performers and seasoned groups — both work. We just need to know which is which so we can place you in the right slot.

What you get:

  • 15-minute slot on a real stage with full lighting and sound.
  • Tech rehearsal before doors. We'll cue you in.
  • Pro video of your performance within 7 days. Use it in your reel, your IG, anywhere.
  • A crowd that wants to be there. Anime, cosplay, idol-adjacent. They cheer. They record. They tag you.
  • Paid? Some slots yes, some are gigs you'd pay for elsewhere — we're upfront on the call.

Drop your demo or reel link in the form. If we don't have a video to watch, we can't book you. That's the only hard rule.
