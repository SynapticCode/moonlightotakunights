PITCH US TO PLAY.

Anime nightlife in Newark, NJ. Monthly QXT's residency, anime con afterparties, and the Mulberry festival on the horizon. If your sound fits, we want to hear you.

I'll be straight with you.

We get pitched constantly. Most of them are random — a DJ with no anime in their feed asking to play an anime night. A producer who's never been to a rave pitching a 2-hour set. Someone who hasn't gigged in a year.

That's not what we're looking for.

Who actually gets booked at Moonlight:

  • DJs whose sound fits the room — anime nightcore, vocal trance, dubstep, happy hardcore, J-core, K-pop edits, future bass, melodic dubstep, electro house. If you've watched a fan-made AMV and thought "I make stuff that sounds like this," we want to hear it.
  • DJs who actually have mixes online — SoundCloud, Mixcloud, YouTube. We listen before we book. No exceptions.
  • DJs who show up early, soundcheck, and don't blow the levels at midnight. Boring stuff. It matters.
  • DJs who promote the night — even one IG story to your followers. We're a team, not a stage rental.
What we pay: We don't pay opening sets for first-time bookings. Headliner and direct support slots are paid — amount depends on the night, the door, and the venue. We're honest about it on the call. No "exposure-only" forever.

What you get out of it:

  • A real anime-nightlife crowd that came specifically to hear this kind of music — not a club where you have to read the room.
  • Pro audio, pro lighting at QXT's. Real venue, real green room.
  • Pro video clips of your set within 7 days. You own the footage.
  • A residency path. Crush your first booking and you're on the list for the next 3 nights.

Drop us your mix link and the rest. We listen to every submission. If your sound fits, you'll hear back within 5 business days.
