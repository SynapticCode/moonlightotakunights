# Mission Statement

**Canonical.** This is the single source of truth for Moonlight Otaku Nights' mission. Any page that needs a mission paragraph pulls it from here, verbatim.

---

## Mission

Moonlight Otaku Nights is anikura built for the otaku and EDM community — where anime, Vocaloid, hardstyle, and everything in between share the same dance floor. Made in collaboration with the DJs, artists, cosplayers, and brands shaping this scene. Our mission is to make beautiful nights people return to in memory long after they end.

## Voice rules (locked from May 17, 2026 conversation)

- No "made with the community, for the community" — slogan-flavored, retired.
- No "as a producer" framing — too corporate for the public-facing mission.
- No negative framing — "none of us could make alone," "not waiting for another party," etc.
- No invented stats (no "400+ fans," no "~500 cap," no "monthly").
- No funnel formulas: no "How to ___ without ___," no em-dash sentence-flips as a default rhythm, no "I'll be straight with you," no "let me keep this short."
- No manufactured scarcity ("5 brands per season," "founding slots fill on a first-applied basis").
- No Russell Brunson value-stack / anchor pricing.
- Anikura is the term. Recurring anikura series, not "monthly residency."
- Venue name (QXT's) stays off public funnel pages until status is locked. Named only on actual event pages.

## Genre scope rule (added May 17, 2026)

**Moonlight is for the otaku AND EDM community.** Anikura is the root, but hardstyle, drum & bass, hardcore, house, Vocaloid, kaigai idol, and the post-monitor goth/industrial crossover are equally Moonlight. Any public-facing mission, hero, or about copy that risks reading anime-only is wrong. When naming genres, always include at least one EDM family alongside anime/Vocaloid so the breadth is unmistakable.

## Place rule (added May 17, 2026)

The canonical mission stays place-agnostic — no Newark, no QXT's, no NJ. The brand is bigger than one venue. Place is named only on actual event pages once the date and venue are locked. This keeps the brand portable as the residency model evolves toward Anime NYC, DerpyCon, AnimeNJ, and beyond.

## Voice direction

- Bilingual nightlife operator. Japanese subheadings stay where they belong (物語, スポンサー募集, etc.). English underneath is plain, warm, written like a real person.
- Beautiful and forward-looking. Never bitter, never negative.
- One claim per paragraph. Trust the noun.
- Specific over abstract — only with facts that are true.
