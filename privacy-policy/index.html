Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 4, 2026

Moonlight Otaku Nights ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit moonlightotakunights.com or attend our events.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Information You Provide

When you interact with us, you may share:

  • Contact Information: Name and email address when joining our mailing list
  • Event Registration: Ticket purchase information (processed by Eventbrite, not stored by us)

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we automatically collect:

  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device type
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time on site, referring website
  • Location Data: General geographic area (city/region level)

Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to understand how our website is used and to measure advertising effectiveness:

  • Analytics: Google Analytics helps us understand website traffic
  • Advertising: Meta Pixel and Google Ads measure campaign performance

HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use collected information to:

  • Send event updates and announcements (only if you've subscribed)
  • Improve our website and user experience
  • Measure marketing effectiveness
  • Communicate about upcoming events

HOW WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION

We do not sell your personal information.

We share information only with service providers who help us operate:

  • Eventbrite — Ticket sales and event management
  • Brevo — Email communications
  • Google — Analytics and advertising
  • Meta — Advertising
  • Hostinger — Website hosting

We may also share information when required by law or to protect our rights.

YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

All Users

You can:

  • Unsubscribe from emails anytime (click "unsubscribe" in any email)
  • Request information about data we have on you
  • Request deletion of your data

California Residents

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have additional rights:

  • Right to Know: Request what personal information we've collected
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt out of the "sharing" of personal information for advertising
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We won't treat you differently for exercising these rights

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

We do not sell personal information for money. However, sharing data with advertising partners (Meta, Google) for targeted ads may qualify as "sharing" under California law.

To opt out:

  • Email info@moonlightotakunights.com with subject line "Opt-Out Request"
  • Enable Global Privacy Control (GPC) in your browser — we honor GPC signals

MANAGING COOKIES

Control cookies through your browser settings. You can also opt out of interest-based advertising:

  • Google: adssettings.google.com
  • Meta: facebook.com/settings/?tab=ads

DATA SECURITY

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. No internet transmission is completely secure, so please use caution when sharing personal information online.

THIRD-PARTY LINKS

Our website links to other sites (Eventbrite, social media, etc.). We're not responsible for their privacy practices — please review their policies.

AGE REQUIREMENT

Our events are 21+ only. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. Contact us immediately if you believe we've collected information from someone underage.

UPDATES

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be posted here with an updated date.
