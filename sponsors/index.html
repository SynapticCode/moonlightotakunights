300+ Expected attendees at our first event
21–35 Core age demographic
100% Anime, gaming & cosplay community

Moonlight Otaku Nights is NJ's first dedicated anikura series — a nightclub format built entirely around anime music, cosplay, and Japanese pop culture. This isn't a one-time event. This is a recurring series with a growing, loyal audience.

The people coming to this event buy anime merch, collect figures, attend conventions, and actively support the brands and businesses that show up in their community. If your business serves this audience — or wants to — this is your room.
