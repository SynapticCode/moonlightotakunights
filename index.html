何これ

A dedicated anikura experience.

You know the feeling — your favorite opening drops and the whole room erupts because they all know it. Someone recognizes your cosplay from that one season nobody watched. You're dancing next to a Chainsaw Man cosplayer and across from someone in full Frieren gear and it just works.

This is anikura — anime club culture. Real nightclub energy built around the music, the characters, and the worlds we actually care about. It's a whole scene in Tokyo and it's spreading worldwide. Now it's here.

Recurring themed nights. Curated DJ sets where every track has a story. Cosplay welcome, never required. Professional sound, real security, anime on every screen. This is home.

🎵

THE SOUND

Anisong remixes, J-Core, happy hardcore, future bass—all rooted in the culture. Every track hits different when you know the source.

⚡

THE ENERGY

Themed nights that go deeper than decoration. Enchanted forests, neon-soaked cityscapes, mecha command centers — each event transforms the space into a world you step into.

🌙

THE CREW

Convention friends. Anime club people. The ones who actually know what "anikura" means. You'll recognize your people immediately.
