I'm Azael Montejo Jr. — most people in this scene know me as Moonlight. I founded Moonlight Productions LLC because the NJ/NYC anime crowd has nowhere to be after 11pm — conventions end, and there is literally no nightlife brand built specifically for them.

So I built one. Here's what's real, and what's next.

What's already proven:

  • Pull radius: Attendees have driven from 90+ miles — NJ, NYC, CT, PA, even DE. Anime fans travel. The market is regional, not local.
  • QXT's residency: Monthly anime night at one of NJ's longest-running alt venues. Repeat attendance is climbing. Cosplay-in is 30–50% of the room.
  • Sponsor traction: Inbound brand interest from apparel, gaming, and energy categories. We screen sponsors with an application funnel — the inverse of "we'll take anyone with a check."
  • Cosplayer-influencer activation: Every cosplay attendee in the 2K–15K-follower range becomes part of our and our sponsors' campaigns through gifted-product handoffs with FTC-compliant disclosure. No other NJ/NYC venue offers this. It's a defensible moat.

What's next:

  • The Mulberry festival — a three-stage outdoor anime/cosplay/EDM festival on Mulberry Commons in Newark. This is the flagship.
  • 4 nights/year minimum at QXT's, scaling to monthly, building toward a recurring brand the way Outside Lands or Camp Flog Gnaw operate — not a one-off promoter.
  • National expansion path: the model travels. Anime/cosplay/EDM crossover audiences exist in every major US metro and they're under-served everywhere.
