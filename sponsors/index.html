Most nightlife sponsorships are logo-on-a-flyer theater. This isn't that.

I've been producing events since the desert compound days in California — 10,000-person warehouse raves. When I came back to NJ, I built Moonlight Otaku Nights from one observation: anime fans are the most loyal consumers in entertainment, and nobody is throwing them a real party.

Conventions get them once a year. Online merch gets them at home. Regular clubs don't get them at all. So we built the only recurring monthly anime · cosplay · rave series in the Northeast. Featured cosplayers, hand-picked DJs, full production, real costumes, real photographers — at QXT's in Newark, the venue that's been the home of Goth/industrial nights on the East Coast for 30 years.

Honest Answers よくある質問

What you're probably thinking right now.

"Why should I sponsor an event series instead of just running Meta ads to this demo?" +
Meta ads put your brand on a screen for 1.7 seconds. Sponsoring Moonlight puts your brand in someone's hands for 4 hours — at the venue, in the photos, on the recap reel, on the cosplayer's feed for months. Plus, anime/cosplay audiences are notoriously hard to target with paid social — they use ad blockers, they cycle accounts, they skip ads. Showing up where they already are, with their permission, is the only reliable way in.
"How do I know you'll actually deliver 400+ people?" +
You don't have to take our word — every contract includes a make-good clause. If actual attendance drops below 80% of projection, we either provide additional social coverage at equivalent market value or apply a credit toward your next event. No promoter at this scale offers that in writing. We do.
"Are these prices firm?" +
No. Tier prices are list — first-event deals close at 60–80% of list while we build a documented track record. Series commitments unlock additional discount. In-kind and media-trade deals are negotiated. Tell us your budget honestly and we'll structure around it.
"What if my brand is alcohol — does that even work in NJ?" +
Yes, but it's structured three-way per NJ ABC tied-house law: (1) Brand pays Moonlight a flat marketing/activation fee. (2) Brand separately routes product through QXT's distributor; QXT's sells through their license. (3) Sampling, branded glassware, pop-up bars, ambassadors on-site are all on the table — coordinated with the venue. We've mapped this out and have legal language ready. Reach out and we'll walk you through it.
"Why an application instead of just pricing on the site?" +
Because half the brands that fill out a sponsor form aren't a fit — wrong demographic, wrong product category, wrong region. Five minutes of qualifying up front means when we hop on a call together, we both already know it's worth the time. You're not pitched. We're built to fit.
"What does the contract actually look like?" +
2-page standard agreement with the 12 industry-standard clauses: term, deliverables, activation rights, category exclusivity, creative approval, payment schedule (50% deposit / 50% 14 days out), make-goods, kill fee, force majeure, IP, indemnification, renewal. We'll send a draft within 48 hours of our call.
"What if I want to start small and grow?" +
Encouraged. Tier 0 and Tier 1 exist exactly for this. Most of our biggest partnerships started as Tier 1 activations — then upgraded after they saw the recap data. We'd rather have you in for $500 this month than not at all.
"When's the next event and what's the timeline?" +
Apply and we'll send the next 3 confirmed dates. Sponsor onboarding takes 2–3 weeks (contract, deposit, creative assets, activation planning). To make the next event, ideally apply at least 4 weeks out.
